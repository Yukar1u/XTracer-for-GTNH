GT API evidence
- gregtech.common.blocks.GTBlockOre exposes getMaterialIndex(int), getStoneIndex(int), getMaterial(int), getStoneType(int), isSmallOre(int), isNatural(int), and GTItemOre.func_77647_b(int) returns the original damage.
- Therefore GT ore item damage is not constrained to world metadata 0..15; the catalog does not truncate it.
